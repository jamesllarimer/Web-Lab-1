Menu

Welcome to a true Appalachian table — where the beans simmer all day, the cornbread’s baked in cast iron (no sugar), and every recipe carries a bit of mountain history. Our menu is rooted in old-time traditions: slow-cooked vittles, hearty fixins, creekside soups, and wild-foraged flavors prepared the right way. We honor the resourcefulness of mountain cooking — simple ingredients, patient methods, and bold, honest flavor. From soup beans and biscuits to moonshine in a jar, everything we serve is made with grit, heritage, and heart. Pull up a chair — supper’s ready.
